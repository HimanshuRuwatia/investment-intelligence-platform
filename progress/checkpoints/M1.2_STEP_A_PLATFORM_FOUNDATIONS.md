## Architectural Decisions

1. **In-house structured logger** — JSON lines to stdout/stderr; injectable `now`/`write` for deterministic tests.
2. **Manual ServiceContainer** — string-token register/resolve; not a DI framework.
3. **Config layout** — checked-in `config/default.json`; TypeScript loader in `src/config/`.
4. **Env overrides** — `NODE_ENV` → `env`, `LOG_LEVEL` → `logging.level`.
5. **Step B deferred** — no Plugin contract, Registry, or Manager in this checkpoint.
